# Teacher Feedback — Mathematics — 2026-08-26

## Evidence reviewed
Wednesday Grade 6 fraction multiplication/division guided practice, independent practice, and exit work.

## What is developing well
- Fraction multiplication procedure is becoming more consistent.
- Atticus can convert some mixed numbers to improper fractions and simplify products.
- He showed useful willingness to write working steps rather than only answers.

## Current gap
Fraction division is not secure. Reciprocal use is inconsistent, especially when mixed numbers are involved. At times the divisor is not inverted correctly or the operation steps become mixed together.

## Teaching decision
Do not accelerate. Continue full Grade 6 instruction. Reteach fraction division briefly before moving deeper into decimal division. Use simple division models first, then mixed-number examples.

## Voice-tutor guidance
When Atticus makes an error, identify exactly which fraction is the divisor and ask, "Which number are we dividing by? Which fraction gets the reciprocal?" Require him to say the keep-change-flip steps in words once, then solve. Do not reveal exit-ticket answers.
