# Grade 6 PE / Wellness — Syllabus + Teaching Guide

## Purpose
Support safe movement, physical literacy, coordination, mobility, strength, endurance, and personal responsibility. PE is scheduled when appropriate; it is not required to appear in every daily packet.

## Strands
- Dynamic warm-up and movement quality
- Coordination and balance
- Speed/agility/reaction
- Strength using age-appropriate bodyweight movements
- Aerobic conditioning
- Mobility/recovery
- Sport skill where relevant
- Goal setting and personal benchmarks

## Session structure
1. Warm-up
2. Skill/movement focus
3. Main conditioning or coordination work
4. Optional sport-specific application
5. Cool-down
6. Record one simple benchmark when useful

## Safety
Technique before intensity. Stop an exercise for pain rather than ordinary exertion and tell a parent. Hydrate appropriately and use safe space/equipment.

## Assessment
Participation/consistency, safe technique, effort, understanding of purpose, and personal improvement. Avoid comparing performance to adult or elite-athlete standards.
