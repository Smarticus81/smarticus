# Grade 6 AR-Weighted Independent Reading Plan - 2026-27

## Purpose
Atticus will complete sustained independent reading alongside daily close reading and writing. The goal is to build reading stamina, vocabulary, inference, literary range, and a school-facing record of completed books.

## Daily Routine
- Read at least 25 minutes on school days.
- If an unfamiliar word does not block meaning, mark it and keep reading.
- If a word blocks meaning, ask Virgil for a one-sentence plain-English definition, reread the sentence, and continue.
- At the end of the session, give one sentence: **What changed or became clearer today?**

## How Points Work
The values below are official Renaissance Accelerated Reader maximum point values used as **book weights** in the homeschool tracker. This homeschool system is separate from Renaissance's official quiz-scoring system.

To bank a book's full weight:
1. Finish the book.
2. Give a 60-second oral summary without notes.
3. Score at least 80% on a fresh homeschool comprehension check.
4. Answer one deeper question about theme, character, author choice, or evidence.
5. If below 80%, review weak areas and complete a new check.

## Virgil Guidance
During independent reading, Virgil should be quiet unless Atticus asks for help. Give short word definitions, not lectures. At the end of a reading block, ask a short no-spoiler conference question. Never claim to administer official Renaissance quizzes. Track the homeschool point goal only after the book-completion process above.
