# Grade 6 Art / Design — Syllabus + Teaching Guide

## Purpose
Develop observation, craftsmanship, visual communication, creativity, critique, and portfolio-quality work through drawing, design, photography, architecture/product thinking, and mixed media.

## Reference
National Core Arts Standards-style goals: create, present, respond, connect.

## Year strands
- Observational drawing and proportion
- Elements/principles of design
- Graphic design and visual identity
- Photography/composition
- Architecture and spatial design
- Product/industrial design thinking
- Digital art where appropriate
- Artist/designer studies from varied cultures and periods
- Critique, revision, and portfolio selection

## Project routine
1. Understand the brief and constraints.
2. Study 2–3 examples/references without copying.
3. Sketch multiple ideas.
4. Choose and explain a direction.
5. Produce a first version.
6. Critique against the brief.
7. Revise craftsmanship/composition.
8. Photograph/scan the final piece and write a short reflection.

## Assessment dimensions
Idea/originality 0–4
Use of visual principles 0–4
Craftsmanship/technical control 0–4
Revision/problem solving 0–4
Reflection/presentation 0–4

## Teaching rules
Do not turn art into coloring worksheets. Demonstrate techniques, then leave meaningful design choices to Atticus. Encourage revision and attention to detail. Themes should vary and can connect to architecture, technology, history, nature, identity, typography, or everyday design.
