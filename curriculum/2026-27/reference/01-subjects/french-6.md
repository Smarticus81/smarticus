# Grade 6 French — Continuing Language Syllabus + Teaching Guide

## Purpose
Continue several years of prior French study toward increasingly independent listening, speaking, reading, and writing. Do not restart as a total beginner.

## Reference
ACTFL World-Readiness communication modes: interpersonal, interpretive, presentational, with cultural comparisons woven into lessons.

## Starting profile
Prior study includes greetings, introductions, numbers, dates, classroom vocabulary, colors/articles, family, likes/dislikes, food, present-tense verbs, and short projects/performances. Grade 5 teacher described strong language aptitude; inconsistent completion sometimes affected grades.

## Year sequence
1. Present tense consolidation: regular -ER verbs, subject pronouns, negation, question forms
2. High-frequency irregular verbs: être, avoir, aller, faire
3. Adjective agreement and description
4. Family, routines, school, interests
5. Food, cafés/restaurants, preferences
6. Time, weather, places, directions, travel
7. Listening/reading short texts and dialogues
8. Guided paragraph writing and short oral presentation
9. Review/cumulative conversation and writing

## Current unit — regular -ER verbs and negation
### Conjugation pattern
parler: je parle, tu parles, il/elle/on parle, nous parlons, vous parlez, ils/elles parlent.
Use the stem + endings: -e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent.

### Negation
Place ne before the conjugated verb and pas after it.
Je parle français. → Je ne parle pas espagnol.
Nous aimons ce film. → Nous n'aimons pas ce film.
Il joue au tennis. → Il ne joue pas au tennis.
Use n' before a vowel sound where appropriate.

## Oral routine
1. Model naturally.
2. Atticus repeats once or twice.
3. Ask a real question.
4. Require a full-sentence answer when appropriate.
5. Correct the highest-value error, explain briefly, and let him repeat.
6. Gradually reduce English support.

## Example questions
Est-ce que tu aimes la musique ?
Est-ce que tu regardes des films ?
Est-ce que tu joues au tennis ?
Qu'est-ce que tu aimes faire après l'école ?

## Writing expectations
Short answers should use complete French sentences when the task calls for them. Prioritize intelligibility, target grammar, and accurate high-frequency forms before stylistic perfection.

## Assessment dimensions
Listening comprehension; spoken response; pronunciation/intelligibility; grammar accuracy; reading comprehension; written accuracy; completion.

## Teaching rules
Do not translate every sentence automatically. Let context work. Do not punish a pronunciation attempt by interrupting constantly. Correct selectively and invite a retry. Cultural content should be accurate and integrated naturally rather than reduced to trivia.
